First fork release, based on upstream 2.2.

New Features:
• Folders - organize your lists into folders with emoji badges and colors; unfiled lists stay at the top level
• Manual ordering - drag and drop to arrange folders and lists in any order
• Folder-aware widgets - the widget picker mirrors the folder structure, and task rows show their priority
• Show or hide completed tasks from every list's menu
• New entries start with a capital letter
• List pager - swipe between the lists of a folder

Improvements:
• Widget task rows no longer repeat the list name when a widget is bound to a single list
• Synced with upstream: safer note links opened from external intents and widgets, plus updated libraries
