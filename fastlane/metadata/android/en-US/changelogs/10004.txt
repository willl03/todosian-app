Fourth fork release, based on upstream 2.2.

Improvements:
• Renaming a folder from the list view's top bar now works while its lists are open - no more "List unavailable" - and returning from the folder view after a rename lands you back where you were
• The colored priority dot moved into the P-chip in the task footer: [(dot) P1] instead of a dot beside the task name
• The section position indicator moved next to the show/hide toggle in the folder bar, with balanced spacing
• The folder card menu on the Home screen now carries icons, matching the folder and section menus
